The part people get wrong

4-way valve

Two halves, one pair each, straight through from IN to OUT.

  • INScrew half: pre-filtered water from stage 3
  • OUTScrew half: on to the membrane
  • INMarked half: clean water from the membrane
  • OUTMarked half: on to the tank and faucet
Tank never fills? The marked half has a one-way check valve inside: you can only blow into its IN. Make sure the membrane’s tube goes there, and never put an IN tube on an OUT.

How the EUR-50 is plumbed

An interactive guide to reconnecting a Bbagua EUR-50 reverse osmosis unit, based on its manual and a unit taken apart.

Real tubes are white, apart from the black drain tube. The coloured band at each end is only a code for matching ends. The cutaways, water colours and the layout under the sink are illustrations.

Not affiliated with Bbagua. Check your unit’s manual before working on it.
